Manual & Explained Method of the Simulation

Working from the logic of supply and demand in real estate development, this simulation explores how policy design could challenge common assumptions. The two main actors are developers and tenants; the simulation acts as the government or policymaker. Adjust the policy blocks below to explore how different rules affect the base condition and the resulting environment. Press play to move through the simulation, or drag the timeline manually. This first version demonstrates the interface with illustrative values. The spatial model and research data will be connected in the next phase.
