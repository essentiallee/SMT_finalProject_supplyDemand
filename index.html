Manual & Explained Method of the Simulation

Explore 20 years of supply, demand, and tenant turnover on a lot. Start with 1,000 units and 800 original households across six provisional Ridgewood income bands. Higher-income applicants arrive over time. Market rent compounds by 8.3% annually, with an additional premium when unmet demand exceeds the threshold. Move policy tiles into Policy Metrics and compare with the no-policy trajectory at the same year. Drag the play button to pause and scrub. Height, coverage, lot merging and FAR change the separate fictional geometry immediately; they do not alter the household model. These are illustrative scenarios, not validated forecasts.
