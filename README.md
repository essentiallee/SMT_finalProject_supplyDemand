Democratizing Policy Simulations:
Working from the preconceived logic of supply-and-demand chain in real estate development, we’re attempting to experiment how policy design could challenge the common belief. 
The two main actors in the simulation are: Developers and Tenants
The simulation acts as the “government” or the policy makers.
Our goal is to not only visualize policy as a tool for reimagining the environment of real estate development, but to also become a generative tool for initiating critique, care, and expanding the application of simulating policies.
